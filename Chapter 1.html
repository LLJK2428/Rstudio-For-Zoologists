What is R?

R is a Free programming language and software designed for statistical analysis, data representation, creating graphs, and so much more. It is one of the most commonly used tools in scientific research because of its ability to efficiently and accurately manage large data sets.

Every command you write is saved in a script, so anyone can repeat the exact analysis months or even years later, making it clear and reproducible. Modern research often involves collecting large amounts of data that would be difficult to manually analyse.

Note

For example, as Zoologists, we may want to investigate

  • Differences in abundances between habitat types
  • Varying parasite loads between populations
  • Identifying populations through Genetic profiles
  • Model population changes over time – incorporating various abiotic and biotic factors
  • Create interactive distribution maps

R allows us to do all of these investigations more efficiently and accurately compared to other software, which may not always be accessible or intuitive.

Note

In summary:

  • A free programming language designed for statistical and data analysis
  • Widely used by zoologists, ecologists and many other scientific fields
  • Allows analysis to be accurately reproduced in the future by anyone
  • Provides a user-friendly interface. Making work a lot more manageable
